Current About

Authority first. Buyer context second.

I coach CEOs and their executive teams through the moments when the company starts growing faster than they are. My clients are founders from seed through Series C. They typically come to me at inflection points: a first-time CEO rapidly scaling, co-founder friction, preparing for a board or a raise, a leadership team growing in scope, or performing well externally while running on fumes.
